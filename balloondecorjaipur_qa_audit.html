Critical — Fix immediately (breaks user experience or trust)
Link

All footer links are broken — point to "#"

Terms & Conditions, Return & Refund, About Us, Disclaimer, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Sitemap — every single one goes to "#". Clicking them does nothing. Legal pages missing is also a compliance risk.

→ Create actual pages or anchor sections, then wire up each link.

CTA

Mobile bottom nav "Call" and "WhatsApp" buttons are dead

The sticky mobile bar has Call and WhatsApp icons but both link to "#". These are your highest-intent CTAs — users tapping them get nothing.

→ Call: href="tel:+917450960060" · WhatsApp: href="https://wa.me/917450960060"

Link

4 of 5 social media footer icons link to "#"

Only Instagram has a real link. Facebook, Twitter/X, YouTube, and one other icon all go to "#" — looks unfinished and unprofessional.

→ Add real profile URLs or remove the dead icons entirely.

Trust

"Loved by 10L+ families" vs "Based on 10+ reviews" — major credibility mismatch

The reviews section simultaneously claims 10 lakh (1,000,000) customers AND only 10+ verified reviews. No real business with a million customers has only 10 reviews. Google and users will notice this immediately.

→ Fix the numbers to be consistent. Either "500+ customers, 10+ reviews" or collect real reviews first.

Trust

Ratings cycle in an obvious repeating pattern — looks fake

Every product card cycles through exactly 4.9 → 4.8 → 4.7 → 4.9 → 4.8 → 4.7. On the birthday category page, review counts repeat the same sequence: 128, 94, 112, 78, 101, 137, 83, 96 — over and over. Real ratings don't look like this.

→ Use real ratings, or at minimum randomize the values so they don't follow a visible loop.

Infra

Logo served from Azure Blob Storage — external dependency risk

Logo loads from camsstorage2026abcd.blob.core.windows.net. If that storage account is deleted, renamed, or has access changed, your logo disappears site-wide with no warning.

→ Upload the logo to your own domain and serve it from there.

High — Affects conversions or SEO significantly
Content

Section heading uses "Moments" twice — "Turning Moments into Everlasting Moments"

The gallery section heading is a typo — it likely should be "Turning Moments into Everlasting Memories".

→ Change second "Moments" to "Memories".

SEO

Identical meta description on every page

Homepage and every category page share the exact same meta description: "India's No.1 decor service in Jaipur…". Google de-ranks pages with duplicate meta tags. Each page needs a unique description.

→ Write unique meta descriptions per page: e.g., "Book birthday balloon decoration in Jaipur starting ₹999. Same-day slots available."

UX

No pricing shown on product cards — only hidden in FAQ

Users browsing the homepage and category pages see "BOOK NOW" with no price. Pricing is mentioned in the FAQ (starts ₹999) but most users never scroll there. Showing starting price increases conversions.

→ Add "Starting ₹999" badge on each card. It removes hesitation before the WhatsApp tap.

Content

Review says "balloon decoration website" — awkward phrasing

First customer review: "I highly recommend this talented balloon decoration website for any event." Real customers don't recommend a "website" — they recommend a "service" or "team".

→ Change "website" to "service" or "team" to sound authentic.

UX

"Car Decoration" section cards have no Book Now button

The "Car Decoration for Wedding" section shows 4 product cards but none have a "BOOK NOW" or "WhatsApp" CTA. Users interested in car decor have no direct path to book.

→ Add WhatsApp BOOK NOW links to each car decoration card, consistent with the rest of the site.

Medium — Image, content, and UX inconsistencies
Images

Kids section images don't match their titles

The 5 "Kids Special" cards on the homepage (Icecream Setup, Butterfly Theme, Cartoon Party, Animal Safari, Candyland) all serve images from a "1st Birthday" folder. The images shown likely don't match the theme names.

→ Use theme-specific images for each card, or verify the correct images are mapped.

Images

Gallery section alt texts don't match the images served

The gallery shows alt text like "Blue birthday ring setup", "Anniversary room decor", etc. — but the actual image URLs are all from the "Baby Shower Decoration" folder. Alt text and images are mismatched.

→ Correct the alt text on each gallery image to match what the image actually shows.

UX

Filter tabs on category page appear decorative (non-functional)

The category page has many filter pills (Premium, Indoor, Quick Setup, Theme Based, etc.) that look interactive but likely don't filter results. Users who tap them expecting to filter will be confused.

→ Either wire up actual filtering, or remove the tabs so the page looks intentional.

Content

Mixed British/American spelling — "Customised" and "Customized" both appear

Both spellings appear as separate product listings ("Customised Birthday Decoration" and "Customized Birthday Decoration"), making them look like duplicate entries rather than intentional SKUs.

→ Pick one spelling and standardize across all listings.

Content

"Sequence" used where "Sequins" is likely meant

Multiple listings say "Sequence Decoration" (e.g., "Rose Gold Birthday Sequence", "Blue Sequence Decoration") — these are almost certainly sequin/shimmer decorations, not number sequences.

→ Search-replace "Sequence" with "Sequins" in decoration product names where relevant.

Improvement — Opportunities to grow trust and conversions
UX

CTA inconsistency — "ORDER NOW" vs "BOOK NOW"

The Corporate Event section uses "ORDER NOW" while every other section uses "BOOK NOW". This small inconsistency breaks the brand voice.

→ Change "ORDER NOW" to "BOOK NOW" for consistency.

SEO

Footer location links go nowhere ("#")

Jaipur, Vaishali Nagar, Mansarovar, Malviya Nagar location links in the footer all go to "#". Location-specific pages are a huge local SEO opportunity.

→ Create area-specific landing pages (e.g., /balloon-decoration-vaishali-nagar-jaipur) and link them.

Trust

"India's No.1 Decor" claim has no source or backing

Claiming "#1 in India" without any reference (award, third-party ranking, etc.) reduces credibility. Visitors know it's unverified marketing.

→ Replace with a more specific, believable claim like "Jaipur's most-booked balloon team" or add a trust badge from a real source.

Conversion

Footer Top Categories links go to "#"

Birthday, Anniversary, Baby Shower, etc. in the footer all link to "#". These are internal SEO links that should point to your real category pages.

→ Link each to its /category/... URL — e.g., Birthday → /category/birthday-decoration
